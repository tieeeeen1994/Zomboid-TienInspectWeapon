title=Tien's Weapon Inspection - WITH GUNSWORKS SUPPORT
description=Hit a key and your character holds up whatever weapon they're carrying and gives it a proper look. A little window tells you how it's holding up.
description=
description=No more digging through your inventory and hovering the mouse just to find out if your axe is about to snap.
description=
description=[h1]What you get[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Press [b]K[/b] to inspect. Rebind it in the options if you like, or use the right-click menu.
description=[*]You'll see condition, sharpness, damage, and how many times it's been repaired. Guns show ammo, whether they're jammed or empty, and how much battery a fitted weapon light or laser has left.
description=[*]Using [b]Gunworks Gang[/b] guns? You'll also see which round is in the chamber and what's loaded in the magazine, so a mixed load of ball and armour-piercing is easy to check. Taped jungle magazines show the side in the gun out of its 30 and the rounds waiting on the other side, and suppressors that wear out show their condition. Guns also show their fire mode (and rate of fire where it can be set), whether the bolt is locked open, whether a folding stock, bipod or bayonet is folded or out, and what is loaded in an underbarrel launcher (or in the rifle, while the launcher is in use). Parts that swap between two states (folding foregrips, rail bipods) show which one is fitted.
description=[*]A fitted weapon light shows whether it is switched on. With [b]Guns of Tien[/b], so does a fitted laser, and a gun with its bayonet in use shows the ammo still loaded in it.
description=[*]Guns show their optimal range: the distances where your shots are most likely to hit, for your Aiming skill and whatever sight is fitted.
description=[*]Axes and hammers show the handle and the head separately, because they wear out at different rates.
description=[*]You can walk around while inspecting. Running or raising your weapon cancels the inspection.
description=[*]Drag the window wherever you like and it opens there next time. The gear in its corner picks which point of the window stays put as it grows and shrinks, and resets it back above the hotbar.
description=[*]Too much on screen? [b]Manage Information[/b] in the gear menu opens a list of checkboxes: untick what you don't need (damage, range, fire mode, batteries...) and the window gets shorter. Remembered for next time. [b]Reset Information[/b] in the same menu shows everything again, without touching where the window sits.
description=[*]You'll need some light. Too dark to read is too dark to inspect.
description=[*]Other players see the animation, so it works in multiplayer.
description=[*]Three ways to inspect, picked with [b]Inspect Mode[/b] in the sandbox settings:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Default[/b]: your character raises the weapon and holds it up while you read. Doing anything else puts it away.
description=[*][b]Persistent[/b] (beta): your character studies the weapon for a moment, then the window opens and stays open through fights, runs and whatever else you get up to, until you close it, press the key again, or put the weapon away.
description=[*][b]Easy[/b] (beta): no animation, no waiting. The window pops up the moment you press the key, always shows whatever is in your hands, and stays until you close it or press the key again.
description=[/list]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Settings[/h1]
description=Inspect Mode (Default, Persistent (beta) or Easy (beta), Default unless changed), how long the inspection takes, and how long your character keeps holding the weapon up. The sandbox page explains each mode right under the mode setting.
description=
description=[h1]Information[/h1]
description=Build 42 only. Safe to add or remove from an existing save. Works with modded weapons, and in whatever language you play in.
