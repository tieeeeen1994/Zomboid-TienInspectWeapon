description=Hit a key and your character holds up whatever weapon they're carrying and gives it a proper look. A little window tells you how it's holding up.
description=
description=No more digging through your inventory and hovering the mouse just to find out if your axe is about to snap.
description=
description=[h1]What you get[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Press [b]K[/b] to inspect. Rebind it in the options if you like, or use the right-click menu.
description=[*]You'll see condition, sharpness, damage, and how many times it's been repaired. Guns show ammo, and whether they're jammed or empty.
description=[*]Axes and hammers show the handle and the head separately, because they wear out at different rates.
description=[*]You can walk around while inspecting. Running or raising your weapon cancels the inspection.
description=[*]You'll need some light. Too dark to read is too dark to inspect.
description=[*]Other players see the animation, so it works in multiplayer.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Settings[/h1]
description=Two configurables: how long the inspection takes, and how long your character keeps holding the weapon up.
description=
description=[h1]Information[/h1]
description=Build 42 only. Safe to add or remove from an existing save. Works with modded weapons, and in whatever language you play in.
